# Capital Media Systems website brand guidelines

Status: **Accepted foundation with proposed website application**

The complete current working standard is `../creative/Brand Guide/dist/index.html`. Supplied artwork under `../creative/Finished Creative Assets/` is authoritative.

## Identity relationship

- Capital Media Systems is the parent, developer, publisher, and owner.
- ChannelDex is the primary product.
- ChannelDex leads in product contexts.
- Capital Media Systems leads in corporate contexts.
- The corporate and product symbols are never merged.

## Corporate mark

The mark uses the Michigan State Capitol dome, custom corporate wordmark, interrupted Capital Blue rule, and the approved tagline **PROGRAMMING A BRIGHTER TOMORROW**. Use the supplied artwork; do not typeset or reconstruct it.

## ChannelDex mark

The symbol is a heavy rounded frame containing three horizontal information records in fixed order: Station Blue, Monitor Green, Broadcast Red. The records may evoke schedule slots, program entries, media records, index cards, channel positions, or a display. They do not represent exactly three television channels.

## Canonical colors

- Charcoal: `#181A1B`
- Capital Blue: `#00529B`
- Station Blue: `#234C74`
- Monitor Green: `#475F43`
- Broadcast Red: `#7D2828`
- Warm Paper: `#F4E4CC`

Canonical sRGB values govern over colors sampled from raster previews.

## Supporting colors proposed for the site

- Near White: `#FFFDFA`
- Warm Line: `#D6C4AA`
- Muted Ink: `#5D5A54`

## Supporting typography

IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono are the current proposed supporting family and are supplied locally. Logos always remain master artwork.

- Sans for display, headings, body, navigation, captions, and controls.
- Mono for short uppercase labels, IDs, timestamps, filenames, archive numbers, and genuinely technical metadata.
- Do not use Mono for ordinary prose.

## Graphic language

Information is the ornament: measured columns, thin rules, schedule structures, record rows, metadata, and modular panels. Use the three-record motif sparingly.

Do not simulate age with distress, yellowing, CRT effects, scan lines, VHS artifacts, terminal flicker, pixel fonts, neon, synthwave, or fake low resolution.

## Corporate-site tension

The approved tagline may carry a faint second reading, but the graphic system remains sincere. Unease belongs in precise language and unexplained continuity, not visual corruption or horror motifs.

## Accessibility

Accessibility takes precedence when strict identity use and legibility conflict. Target WCAG 2.2 AA, preserve visible focus, and never rely on color alone.

## Logo handling

- Do not stretch, crop, rotate, mirror, skew, recolor, outline, shadow, bevel, or glow.
- Do not simulate reversed artwork with filters.
- Keep at least 1X clear space around compact ChannelDex symbols and 1.5X around full lockups.
- Do not place text, controls, borders, photographs, or competing marks within clear space.
- Preserve the official rules and endorsements contained in supplied artwork.

## Current caveats

The current Brand Guide is Version 1.1 Draft. It contains an “open asset work” table that still calls product-only masters needed even though those PNG and Illustrator files are present in the same current creative tree. Treat the supplied masters as present; do not infer that every governance note has been editorially reconciled.
