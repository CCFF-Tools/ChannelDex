## Voice checklist

- Direct, measured, and specific
- Slightly dry rather than playful
- Optimistic without promotional excess
- Technical only when the detail improves understanding
- No unsupported superlatives
- No SaaS clichés such as “revolutionary,” “seamless,” or “single pane of glass”
- No overt menace, conspiracy, or horror language
- No false certainty around plans, readiness, or broadcast history
