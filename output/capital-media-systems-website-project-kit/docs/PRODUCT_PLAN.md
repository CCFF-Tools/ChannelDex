# Capital Media Systems corporate website product plan

Status: **Proposed**

## Purpose

Create the public corporate presence for Capital Media Systems and establish ChannelDex as its primary product. The website should communicate maturity, operational competence, and long-term stewardship while remaining independent of proprietary hosting platforms.

## Audience

- Public-media and community-television station staff
- Programming and operations personnel
- Technical and administrative decision-makers
- Prospective ChannelDex users
- Partners or vendors seeking basic company context
- People researching prior Capital Media Systems products

## User needs

Visitors should be able to:

1. Understand what Capital Media Systems does.
2. Recognize ChannelDex as its flagship product.
3. Understand ChannelDex's real scope without inflated automation claims.
4. Learn the proposed company history and product lineage once approved.
5. Find a verified way to contact the company.
6. Access privacy and accessibility information.

## Proposed routes

- `/` — corporate home
- `/channeldex/` — flagship product
- `/systems/` — current and historical systems
- `/company/` — company history and principles
- `/contact/` — verified contact path
- `/privacy/` — privacy statement
- `/accessibility/` — accessibility commitment
- `/404.html` — branded recovery page

## Experience principles

- Information becomes the ornament.
- Corporate identity leads globally; ChannelDex leads within product context.
- Historical character comes from discipline, not simulated age.
- The site is legible and complete without motion or JavaScript.
- The unsettling secondary reading is optional and never reduces credibility.
- Public claims stay within verified product and company truth.

## Initial deliverables after approval

- Responsive static website for all proposed routes
- Local fonts and optimized brand derivatives
- Designed image placeholders
- Page metadata, sitemap, and robots file
- Accessible navigation and focus behavior
- Print styles
- Local preview documentation
- Caddy and nginx configuration examples
- Automated structural/link checks
- Manual visual, keyboard, responsive, and accessibility QA

## Explicit exclusions

- Content-management system
- Database
- Accounts or authentication
- Analytics and tracking
- Third-party widgets
- E-commerce
- Licensing portal
- Working contact form without an approved backend contract
- Unsupported integrations or product claims
- Invented customers, awards, certifications, staff, addresses, or testimonials

## Success criteria

- Visitors can explain the relationship between Capital Media Systems and ChannelDex after viewing the home page.
- ChannelDex feels current, durable, and continuously maintained.
- Capital Media Systems feels broader and older than one product without relying on fake nostalgia.
- The website deploys from ordinary static files without required external services.
- WCAG 2.2 AA requirements and the validation plan pass.
- No proposed or open claim is published as accepted fact.
