# Accepted: browser channel workspaces (2026-10-06)

The active station is selected in a compact browser-session selector and scopes
station-owned pages, forms, queues, schedules, library records, and device
settings. PUB-TV is the canonical legacy fallback. Devices belong to exactly
one station; legacy devices are assigned to PUB-TV and names are unique within
that station. Historical records retain their original device provenance.
