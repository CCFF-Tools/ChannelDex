Shows · explanatory detail

Premiere cycle rules

Reference information for the schedule decisions in this proposal.

Harbor Lights

Premieres are the boundary for an episode’s replay cycle. A replay earlier in the calendar week can still belong to the prior premiere. Producer direction controls intended order; a selected episode or “No program” choice wins over carry-forward.

Current example

Later cycle

Ancillary areas

History retains planned records and provenance separately from evidence that a day aired. Settings can hold the owner’s standing carry-forward authorization and time zone. They are intentionally described here rather than presented as operational controls.

Why Shows is in the primary navigation It explains the cycle rule at the moment a person needs it. History and Settings remain ancillary because they support review and policy without competing with today’s actions.

Audit notes

This prototype tests the proposed information architecture with synthetic records.

  • Fixed program starts stay fixed.
  • Full-day planning includes IDs, PSAs, fillers, and live entries.
  • Preparation, planning, and airing evidence stay distinct.
  • Every visible action is reversible or illustrative.
