Help

Complete your station's work

Start with the video, confirm details and airtimes, and follow preparation and publication from the same record.

Bring in media

Choose Add media from Work or Library. Select videos where they already live. ChannelDex inspects local files without copying source media. Review suggested show, title, episode number, and premiere/replay dates. Resume unfinished intake from Work.

Preparation uses configured tools and qualified destination settings. Planning remains available while media is being prepared. Missing configuration and failed work remain visible; a saved episode does not mean it reached the playback device.

Find an episode and its dates

Library contains shows, episodes, and reusable station IDs, PSAs, and filler. Open an episode to see upcoming premiere and replay dates and times first. Suggested dates are not saved plans. Files, preparation, publication, and history remain with that episode.

Correct episode numbers or producer information there. A corrected video belongs to the existing episode as a new version. Older media and historical evidence are retained.

Change the schedule

Day, Week, and Agenda show the same plan. Change one play or review a premiere/replay cycle from the episode. One-off changes remain exceptions. Recurring times changes preview affected future plays from an effective date.

Use Add to schedule for live entries and other timed items. Fixed starts, intentional virtual-channel intervals, and unknown gaps remain distinct. ChannelDex does not shift, trim, add filler, or infer airing automatically.

Publish and verify

Review pending changes shows the selected device, plays, and blockers. Qualified preparation and a current review are required before staging or activation. Sent, activated, and independently verified are different outcomes; none is proof of airing.

Preparation activity retains job details and retry controls. Locate missing files from their episode; changed bytes need a new version and fresh review. Completed transfer evidence remains intact.

History and maintenance

History is a read-only record of schedule uploads, airing evidence, and audit events. Preparation and schedule records do not imply that an item aired. Record reports or log verification from the affected scheduled play.

Archive records after resolving future uses and active jobs. Restore does not recreate cancelled schedules. Administration holds exceptional no-file episode entry.

Settings

Configure the channel, America/Detroit timezone, and playback devices in Settings. Credentials stay in the local secret store. ChannelDex does not fetch private submissions or require cloud hosting.
