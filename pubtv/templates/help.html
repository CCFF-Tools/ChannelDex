Help

How to use ChannelDex

Plan the whole broadcast day, record manual media work, and keep airing evidence separate from intent.

Start here

Set up Station Settings, add devices from Devices, then build the catalog in Shows. The V1 station boundary is PUB-TV and times use America/Detroit.

Use Today for current attention items. A plan, preparation note, or upload record is not proof that an item aired.

Plan a premiere cycle

  1. In Shows, open a show and choose Plan premieres from its workspace.
  2. Choose the concrete premiere date and episode. The next pending episode is suggested; choose an older episode explicitly when needed, or choose no program.
  3. Review the generated premiere and replay cycle. A replay before the next premiere belongs to the prior cycle even when it is in an earlier calendar week.

Prepare the complete day

Open Day, Week, or Agenda. Choose Add to schedule for an episode, station ID, PSA, filler, live event, or other item. Enter date/time as 2026-10-07T19:00 in Eastern time and duration as hours/minutes. Starts are fixed; ChannelDex flags overlaps, short gaps, and over-slot episodes but does not shift or trim them.

Recurring times are reservations, not evidence of airing. An explicit episode or No program choice wins over any station carry-forward default.

Prepare media and review delivery

Drop source files into Media Preparation, choose a show and destination device, select each file's episode (or create a new episode), and choose whether to encode before transfer. Asset preparation records source availability, AME preset/version/machine/output, and exact destination transfer or WinLGX registration.

On Review schedule, select a destination, check each cycle, choose its exact Episode video, then choose Review changes. Read Playback, Review, Existing media, and Controller mappings. If replacements appear, check the authorization box before Confirm reviewed schedule plan. A fresh review is required after schedule, assignment, recurrence, asset, or destination changes; review/confirmation does not upload or activate.

Use Schedule delivery only for the attended, qualification-gated workflow. Approval does not control playout.

Record evidence

Use History and an occurrence workbench to record airing evidence. Choose exactly one status: Reported/observed or Log verified. Include the source, date/time, notes, and any correction relationship. Scheduled status is separate from both airing-evidence choices.

Settings and diagnostics

Station Settings contains the PUB-TV name, America/Detroit timezone, and bounded carry-forward/publication defaults. A device stores its host, FTP port (normally 21), media directory (for example /Vol1/mpeg), command port (normally 23), local AME/FFmpeg paths, and private qualification evidence paths. The schedule path is fixed at /internal/schedule/schedule.bin.

On Settings, choose a device's Configure device link to run bounded read-only FTP, AME, FFmpeg, or evidence-file diagnostics. Results are observational and stale as soon as settings change; they do not qualify a target or imply automatic upload, airing, or playout.
