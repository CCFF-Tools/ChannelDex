Help

How the workflow fits together

ChannelDex records a complete plan and the evidence collected while preparing and delivering it.

Plan and schedule

Drop a delivered source into the media queue to prepare it. Use Plan show premieres for recurring episode premieres and replays. Use Add one-time item for a station ID, PSA, filler, live event, or other single schedule item. A planned item is not proof that it aired.

Prepare and review

Media preparation records inspection, encoding notes, transfer details, and WinLGX registration. Review schedule checks the selected occurrence revision and produces a preview. It does not activate a controller.

Delivery

Delivery is an attended, prepare-only exchange. The device page can test connection and local tools without writing to the controller. Upload and activation remain explicit workflow actions.

Queues and history

Episode queues track intended order and premiere planning. History records reported airing evidence separately from the complete planned day. Technical qualification is read-only on ordinary device saves; observed fingerprints can become stale and require explicit review.
