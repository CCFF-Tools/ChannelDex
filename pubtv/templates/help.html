How to use ChannelDex

Plan the broadcast day, record media preparation, and keep airing evidence separate from intent.

Media Preparation

Open Media Preparation, choose one show and destination, then use Add episode files or paste an absolute local path. Review each row's title, existing episode, optional number, runtime, and encoding mode. Confirm once to add the library records and queue preparation. Source files stay in place.

Use Library to search by title or number, filter show and readiness, inspect versions and runtime provenance, and plan one episode or an ordered same-show selection.

Schedule

Use Today, Week, or Agenda to plan the complete broadcast day, including episodes, filler, station IDs, PSAs, and live events. Starts remain fixed and planned coverage is distinct from airing evidence.

Plan premiere cycles from Shows or Library after media intake. Add items at new occurrence, review recurring schedule, and use schedule preparation and schedule delivery for reviewed handoff.

Relink missing local media

In Library, choose or paste a replacement path and Review relink. The background worker compares the complete file. Confirm matching location only after its fingerprint matches. Different or unknown bytes require Review this file as a new version; prior runtime and verification are not inherited. Active preparation must finish first. A moved input needs fresh review before queued work can resume, while completed transfer history remains available.

Delivery and evidence

Record source availability, encoding notes, exact transfer details, and library registration manually. Use an occurrence workbench or History to record reported or log-verified airing evidence. Preparation and schedule records do not imply that an item aired; evidence is not proof that an item aired unless independently recorded. Reported/observed and Log verified are the available evidence states.

Playback, Review, Existing media, and Controller mappings remain explicit records. Confirm reviewed schedule plan only after review; do not qualify a target through documentation alone.

Settings

Configure the active station, America/Detroit timezone, and playback devices under Settings. Manage device details from Playback devices. Credentials remain in the local secret store; ChannelDex does not fetch private submissions or require cloud hosting.
